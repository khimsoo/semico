Construction Services

Cleanroom Build

Turnkey cleanroom construction — from planning and design to ISO certification and ongoing operations.

Our Process

Six-Phase Delivery

A structured approach ensuring ISO-compliant cleanroom delivery on time and within budget.

1

Planning & Requirements

Define project scope, ISO class level, utilities. Assess facility space, workflow, and contamination control. Set lead time and compliance standards.

2

Design & Engineering

Architectural layout for cleanroom zones. HVAC and filtration system design. Utility integration — DIW, PCW, gases, electrical, waste treatment.

3

Construction

Build cleanroom structure — walls, flooring, ceiling, airlocks. Install HVAC, filtration, and pressure control. Set up DIW/PCW pipelines and waste treatment.

4

Equipment Installation

Deliver and install semiconductor tools and cleanroom equipment. Connect all utilities — water, cooling, gases, power. Validate equipment performance.

5

Testing & Validation

Perform cleanroom certification to ISO standards. Test DIW, PCW, and waste treatment systems. Validate airflow, particle counts, and environmental stability.

6

Operations & Maintenance

Ongoing monitoring of cleanroom performance. Preventive maintenance for utilities and equipment. Continuous improvement for efficiency and shorter lead times.
