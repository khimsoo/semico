Our Expertise

Full-Spectrum Fire Suppression & Detection

SemiCo's fire protection division delivers end-to-end services across Singapore's semiconductor, pharmaceutical, and industrial sectors. From initial site assessment through to annual regulatory testing, our team manages the full lifecycle of fire safety systems — so your facility remains compliant and protected without any operational disruption.

All works are carried out in accordance with SCDF (Singapore Civil Defence Force) requirements and the relevant Singapore Standards, supported by personnel with over 30 years of hands-on industry experience.

Our Process

How We Work

A structured, end-to-end approach that ensures every project is delivered safely, on schedule, and fully compliant.

1

Site Survey & Assessment

We conduct a thorough on-site assessment to evaluate your facility's risk profile, existing infrastructure, and applicable SCDF code requirements before any work begins.

2

Design, Installation & Commissioning

Our qualified team designs and installs suppression, detection, and alarm systems to specification — followed by full functional commissioning and documentation for authority submission.

We provide structured preventive maintenance programmes and conduct annual inspections and testing to ensure continued compliance and operational readiness.
